1. Who we are

Kaselca Apps is the trading name of Mike Harvey G. Ambait, an individual developer based in Can-avid, Eastern Samar, Philippines ("Kaselca Apps", "we", "us", "our"). We are the controller of personal information processed through our apps, except where a third-party service acts as an independent controller, as described below.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps and games published by Kaselca Apps on Google Play (the "Apps"). It explains what information is collected when you use the Apps, how it is used, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

3. Information we collect

Information stored on your device

Game progress, unlocked content, purchased items, scores and settings are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Information collected by advertising partners

Apps that show ads use Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • device and network information, such as device model, operating system version, language, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • interactions with ads, such as impressions, clicks and rewarded-ad completions;
  • diagnostic information used to detect fraud and measure ad performance.

Purchase information

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details. We receive only the information needed to deliver and verify your purchase, such as the product purchased, an order ID and the purchase status. We never receive your card number or billing details.

Leaderboards (where available)

If an App offers an online leaderboard and you choose to take part, we collect the nickname you enter and your scores or times so they can be shown to other players. Please don't use your real name or other personal information as a nickname.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

What we don't collect

The Apps do not ask for your name, contact list, photos, precise GPS location, microphone or camera access.

5. Third-party services

The Apps rely on these third-party services, which process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Play (distribution, payments and billing): Google Privacy Policy

AdMob may share ad requests with other certified ad partners through Google's ad mediation and auction systems. The full list of Google's ad technology providers is shown in the consent message where applicable.

6. Ads and your choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: before personalised ads are shown, the App displays a consent message powered by Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept, reject or manage your choices, and change them later from the App's settings menu where a privacy option is provided.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Once deleted, apps can no longer use it for personalised ads.
  • Non-personalised ads: if you decline consent, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised based on your activity.
  • Remove ads: some Apps offer a purchase that removes ads.

7. Children

The Apps are designed for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent where you live, which is up to 16 in some EEA countries). If you believe a child has provided personal information through an App, for example a leaderboard nickname that contains their real name, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data stays until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Leaderboard nicknames and scores are kept while the leaderboard is active. You can ask us to remove your entry at any time.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you, and generally no longer than 2 years.
  • Third-party services such as AdMob and Google Play keep data according to their own retention policies.

To request deletion of any data we hold about you, email us using the address below. Because you don't create an account with us, please tell us which App you use and, for leaderboards, your nickname.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information deleted;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • complain to a data protection authority.

These rights apply under laws including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR. In the Philippines, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission. In the EEA or UK, you can contact your local data protection authority. We will respond to requests within the time limits set by applicable law.

10. International transfers

We are based in the Philippines, and our service providers, including Google, may process information in other countries, including the United States. When information about users in the EEA or UK is transferred, it is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses used by Google.

11. Security

We keep the information we handle to a minimum and use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for any online features. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a data breach where the law requires it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when we add new features or third-party services, or when the law changes. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how your information is used, we will give notice in the relevant Apps where reasonably possible.
